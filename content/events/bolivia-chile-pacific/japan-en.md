Japan steadfastly champions the **rule of law** and the peaceful settlement of international disputes through multilateral judicial institutions. Tokyo welcomed the peaceful resolution framework demonstrated by both nations in submitting the dispute to the International Court of Justice (ICJ) and supports the implementation of the 2018 judgment as a vital element of international legal certainty in Latin America.

Chile represents one of Japan's most indispensable economic partners in the Pacific Rim as a critical supplier of **copper**, **molybdenum**, and **lithium**, reinforced by the Japan-Chile EPA and the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership (CPTPP). Simultaneously, Japan maintains long-standing ties with Bolivia through JICA infrastructure and technological cooperation on Uyuni salt flat lithium processing. Japan advocates pragmatic border modernization and regional transit integration that foster economic prosperity without destabilizing diplomatic relations.
