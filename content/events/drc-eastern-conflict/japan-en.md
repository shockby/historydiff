The Government of Japan approaches the protracted instability in eastern DRC through its foundational commitment to the rule of law, national sovereignty, and the peaceful resolution of conflicts through diplomatic dialogue. Through the **Tokyo International Conference on African Development (TICAD)** framework, Japan champions the concept of "Human Security," extending sustained multi-million-dollar emergency humanitarian relief and developmental aid through international bodies (including UNHCR, WFP, and IOM) to support internally displaced persons, restore healthcare and sanitation, and strengthen judicial and civilian policing capacities.

From an industrial and economic vantage point, as a major manufacturing powerhouse dependent on critical minerals for advanced electronics, automotive components, and battery technology, Japan places supreme importance on eliminating **conflict minerals** (tantalum, tin, tungsten, and gold—3TG) from global supply chains. Japan actively promotes corporate adherence to the **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains**, backing the International Conference on the Great Lakes Region (ICGLR) certification mechanism. Tokyo urges all regional state actors to refrain from destabilizing unilateral interventions and to foster transparent, verified trade architectures that uplift local populations.
