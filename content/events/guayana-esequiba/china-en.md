China adopts a balanced diplomatic stance grounded in the Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence, urging Venezuela and Guyana to resolve their historic boundary dispute through friendly bilateral consultations and peaceful dialogue. While Beijing shares an "all-weather strategic partnership" with Venezuela, it simultaneously expands economic cooperation with Guyana under the **Belt and Road Initiative**, emphasizing non-interference and regional equilibrium.

Crucially, state-owned **China National Offshore Oil Corporation (CNOOC)** holds a lucrative **25%** working interest in the offshore Stabroek block alongside ExxonMobil and Hess. Because any cross-border confrontation would directly jeopardize substantial Chinese energy investments and offshore logistics, Beijing consistently advocates restraint and dialogue under the Argyle framework, while remaining critical of increased US military operations in the region.
