Japan firmly adheres to the **rule of law** in the international community and opposes any unilateral attempts to change the status quo by force or coercion. In response to Venezuela's December 2023 referendum and subsequent legislative maneuvers, Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed grave concern and called for full compliance with the provisional measures indicated by the International Court of Justice (ICJ), urging a peaceful resolution through diplomatic engagement.

Tokyo maintains extensive development cooperation with Guyana and members of the Caribbean Community (CARICOM) under the Japan-CARICOM framework, collaborating on maritime safety, infrastructure, and environmental resilience. From the perspective of global maritime trade and international legal predictability, Japan stresses that international borders must be respected and that Latin America and the Caribbean remain a designated "zone of peace."
