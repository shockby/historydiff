The United States, the United Kingdom, and Western allies strongly support Guyana's sovereign territorial integrity within its 1899 borders. Historically, Washington invoked the **Monroe Doctrine** in 1895 to compel Britain into arbitration, resulting in the 1899 Paris Award. Anglo-American diplomacy regards the arbitration outcome as a binding, final legal settlement, rejecting Caracas's revisionist claims as unfounded.

With major American energy investments led by **ExxonMobil** transforming Guyana into an essential Western energy hub, Washington views Venezuelan aggression as a direct threat to hemispheric security. The US Southern Command (SOUTHCOM) has intensified security cooperation with the Guyana Defence Force through joint aerial maneuvers and intelligence sharing, while the UK dispatched Royal Navy patrol vessels to Georgetown, underscoring decisive deterrence against any military aggression.
