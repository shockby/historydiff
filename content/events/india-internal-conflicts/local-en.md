Human rights groups among **Adivasi (Indigenous tribal) communities** in central India's forests say anti-Naxal campaigns are tied to mining and development and have left villagers caught between both sides, killed, detained or displaced. The state-backed vigilante force **Salwa Judum** (from 2005) was ruled unconstitutional by the Supreme Court in 2011. In the Northeast, many demand repeal of AFSPA, arguing that broad immunity for soldiers breeds impunity; in 2004 women in Manipur marched naked in protest against the Act. **Sikhs** note that in the 1984 riots about **2,800** people were officially killed in Delhi alone (civil society says over 3,000), yet convictions took decades. **Muslims** point to the 2002 Gujarat riots, where **over 1,000** died (mostly Muslims) and prosecutions were seen as inadequate, and to “bulldozer” demolitions and the response to the 2020 Delhi riots (**53** dead) as evidence of unequal treatment. In Manipur, **Kuki-Zo** groups say that **over 200** died and about 60,000 were displaced after 2023, accuse the state government of bias, and demand a separate administration with a legislature.
