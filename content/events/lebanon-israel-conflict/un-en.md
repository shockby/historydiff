The United Nations has maintained UNIFIL (UN Interim Force in Lebanon) in southern Lebanon since 1978, tasked with monitoring the cessation of hostilities and protecting civilians. Security Council Resolution 425 demanded Israel's complete withdrawal from Lebanon; the Blue Line was demarcated upon Israel's withdrawal in 2000. Resolution 1701, adopted after the 2006 war, mandated an enhanced UNIFIL and the deployment of Lebanese Armed Forces to the south.

The UN position calls on all parties to respect the Blue Line and cease unilateral military action. Hezbollah's disarmament is required under Resolutions 1559 and 1701, but implementation remains incomplete. On Shebaa Farms, the UN treats the area as part of the Israeli-occupied Golan Heights, not Lebanese territory, keeping the question disputed. UNIFIL personnel have repeatedly faced hostile acts from both sides, making force protection a persistent operational challenge.
