The United States strongly supports Israel's right to self-defense and designates Hezbollah as an Iran-backed Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO). The 1983 Beirut barracks bombing, which killed 241 US Marines, remains foundational to American enmity toward Hezbollah. During the 2006 war, Washington backed Israeli military operations before ultimately supporting the UN-brokered ceasefire under Resolution 1701.

US policy calls on Lebanon to disarm Hezbollah and reduce its political influence while providing parallel support to the Lebanese Armed Forces to strengthen state institutions. Washington broadly aligns with Israel's position that Shebaa Farms is part of the Golan Heights and supports UNIFIL monitoring. The Lebanon-Israel conflict is typically framed within the broader context of countering Iranian regional expansion and its "Axis of Resistance." US officials view Hezbollah's integration into Lebanese politics as a fundamental obstacle to state sovereignty and regional stability.
