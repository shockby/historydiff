China values Libya's oil resources and its strategic access to the Mediterranean and Africa. It opposed the 2011 NATO intervention, viewing it as the root cause of the current instability. Beijing maintains dual-track diplomacy, keeping contact with both the GNU in the west and the LNA (General Haftar) in the east. China is cautiously watching developments with an eye on Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) expansion and infrastructure investments, and has not sent military forces.
