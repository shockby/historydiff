The Government of National Unity (GNU) is the UN-recognized legitimate government based in Tripoli. It relies heavily on Turkish military training and support, and holds a financial advantage through its management of oil revenues. However, its actual control is limited due to the proliferation of militias. While nominally advocating for elections and national unity, it prioritizes maintaining its power base and is engaged in UN-mediated negotiations with the eastern LNA.
