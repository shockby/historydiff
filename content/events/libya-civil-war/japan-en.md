Japan maintains an economic interest in Libya's oil resources and energy security but has not engaged in military intervention. It politically supports the UN-led peace process and contributes to the funding of UNSMIL. Japan's primary practical concern is protecting the oil and gas interests of Japanese trading companies and energy firms, hoping for stable governance and early democratic elections.
