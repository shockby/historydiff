The United States participated in the 2011 NATO intervention (Operation Odyssey Dawn) that toppled the Gaddafi regime, but scaled back direct involvement as instability deepened. While continuing occasional airstrikes against ISIS remnants, the US is highly concerned about the expanding influence of Russia (Wagner Group), Turkey, the UAE, and Egypt, viewing Libya as an arena of geopolitical competition. The US supports UN mediation efforts.
