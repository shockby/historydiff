For Iran, these eight decades begin as a struggle against Western subjugation under the Pahlavi dynasty. The **1953 coup that ousted Prime Minister Mosaddegh** — orchestrated by CIA and MI6 in "Operation Ajax" — stands as the foundational evidence that a democratically elected leader who dared to protect Iran's oil resources could be toppled by imperial powers. It is the wellspring of Iranian anti-American and anti-British consciousness.

The **1979 Islamic Revolution**, under Imam Khomeini's leadership, is understood as the greatest popular revolution of the 20th century — sweeping away the Pahlavi dictatorship and American interference in one blow. The **seizure of the U.S. Embassy and hostage crisis** that November was a legitimate act of resistance against a "den of spies." In the **Iran-Iraq War of 1980–88**, Iran waged a national "Sacred Defense (دفاع مقدس)" against Saddam Hussein's aggression, even as the United States, Western Europe, and Arab oil states backed Iraq.

Iran does not recognize the "Zionist regime" and, through its support for the Axis of Resistance — Hezbollah, Hamas, and the Houthis — considers itself the guardian of Palestinian liberation. Nuclear development is Iran's **right to peaceful nuclear energy guaranteed by the NPT**, and Iran maintains that the JCPOA (2015) was destroyed by the United States' unilateral withdrawal in 2018.

The **Israeli-American strikes on Iran's nuclear facilities in June 2025** constitute what Iran defines as an act of aggression tantamount to a declaration of war. Iran's retaliatory measures — including threats to close the Strait of Hormuz and mass ballistic missile launches — are legitimate exercises of the right to self-defense under international law. Supreme Leader Khamenei declared that "the resistance will continue," firmly rejecting any capitulation to the United States or Israel.
