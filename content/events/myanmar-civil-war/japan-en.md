Since the February 2021 military coup, Japan has consistently demanded that Myanmar's military (Tatmadaw) immediately cease violence, release all political prisoners including Aung San Suu Kyi, and swiftly restore a democratic system. Japan expressed "serious concern" over the military-staged elections held in 2025–26, refusing to recognize their legitimacy.

While suspending government-to-government development aid, Japan has continued and expanded humanitarian support through international organizations and NGOs (approximately ¥3.7 billion in additional aid in early 2024). Japan provides maximum support for ASEAN's Five-Point Consensus (5PC) and, in coordination with fellow ASEAN partners, maintains pressure on the junta at the United Nations. As a key partner of Southeast Asia, the Myanmar crisis has become one of Japan's most pressing regional diplomatic challenges.
