The armed conflict that erupted in April 2023 between the **Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF)** and the **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)** has become one of the world's worst humanitarian crises of the 21st century. Under the banner of "human security," Japan has continuously provided humanitarian aid through UN agencies (WFP, UNICEF, UNDP) and international NGOs.

Japan has refrained from military involvement, consistently supporting a diplomatic resolution. It has channeled funding through **IGAD (Intergovernmental Authority on Development)** to support regional mediation, promoting ceasefires and dialogue. Through G7 joint statements, Japan has called on the international community to ensure civilian protection and humanitarian access. Japan has also strengthened cooperation with African nations via TICAD (Tokyo International Conference on African Development), with the restoration of stable governance and democratic institutions in Sudan as a key diplomatic objective.
