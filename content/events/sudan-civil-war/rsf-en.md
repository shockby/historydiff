The **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)** claim that the April 2023 fighting was triggered by the SAF's preemptive actions. Originally rooted in the Darfur conflict's Janjaweed militia, the RSF—under the command of Hemedti (Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo)—retains broad de facto control over western Sudan and the Darfur region. Reportedly backed by foreign actors including the UAE, the RSF has continued drone strikes and attacks against SAF-controlled areas.

While the RSF publicly advocates for a "legitimate government" and inclusive political dialogue, the international community has widely condemned its large-scale massacres of ethnic and religious minorities in Darfur, sexual violence, and forced displacement as tantamount to genocide. In July 2026, a Sudanese court sentenced Hemedti to death in absentia, a verdict the RSF has dismissed as illegitimate.
