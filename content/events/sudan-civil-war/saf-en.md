The **Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF)** characterize the April 2023 conflict as a "rebellion against the state," claiming the **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)** launched an unprovoked first strike. As Sudan's legitimate national military, the SAF frames the conflict as a fight to restore constitutional order and national unity. By 2026, strategic counter-offensives in North Kordofan recaptured key supply routes, breaking the RSF's encirclement and reasserting military advantage.

The SAF and Sudanese government have urged the international community to recognize RSF attacks on civilians, widespread looting, and sexual violence as genocide. In July 2026, a Sudanese court sentenced RSF leader Hemedti (Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo) and 15 others to death in absentia for war crimes and crimes against humanity. The SAF has prioritized a military resolution over negotiations, maintaining a cautious stance toward international ceasefire mediation.
