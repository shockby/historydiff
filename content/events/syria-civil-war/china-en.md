Throughout the **Syrian Civil War**, China has advocated for non-interference and political solutions, frequently partnering with Russia to veto UN Security Council sanctions against the Assad regime. Beijing has consistently positioned the Syrian crisis as a prime example of Western interference causing regional instability.

China holds significant economic interests in Syria's oil and gas sectors, as well as its long-term reconstruction. Following the collapse of the Assad regime in late 2024, China remains cautious toward the new **HTS** government due to historical terrorism concerns. However, Beijing actively seeks economic entry into the national reconstruction process to expand its influence in the Middle East.
