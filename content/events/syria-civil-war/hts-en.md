Having seized power and ousted the Assad regime in late 2024, the new **HTS (Hayat Tahrir al-Sham)**-led government has formally declared its break from al-Qaeda. The new administration advocates for "moderate Islamic governance" and is actively seeking dialogue with Western nations to establish legitimacy and demand the immediate lifting of economic sanctions.

The new government publicly claims to protect the rights of minority groups, including Christians, Kurds, and Alawites. Moving forward, **HTS** faces immense challenges, including the integration of diverse armed factions, facilitating the safe return of millions of refugees, and spearheading a massive national reconstruction effort after over a decade of devastating conflict.
