Since the outbreak of the **Syrian Civil War** in 2011, Japan has consistently called for a diplomatic resolution. While strongly condemning the Assad regime's use of chemical weapons, Japan maintained diplomatic ties with the government. Furthermore, Japan has provided tens of billions of yen in humanitarian aid to support Syrian refugees, fulfilling its international responsibilities.

Following the collapse of the Assad regime in late 2024 by an offensive led by **HTS (Hayat Tahrir al-Sham)**, Japan is carefully monitoring the political transition. Japan is exploring the possibility of reviewing HTS's terrorist designation and considers future participation in Syria's reconstruction as a key policy goal to stabilize the Middle East.
