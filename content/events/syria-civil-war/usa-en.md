During the **Syrian Civil War**, the United States launched **Operation Inherent Resolve** to defeat **ISIS**, heavily backing the Kurdish-led **SDF (Syrian Democratic Forces)**. The U.S. maintained strict sanctions against the Assad regime through the **Caesar Act** to limit the expanding influence of Russia and Iran in the region.

With the recent transition to an **HTS**-led government, the U.S. is cautiously evaluating the group's Islamist tendencies while considering partial sanctions relief. Washington continues to prioritize eliminating Iranian and Hezbollah presence in Syria to ensure Israel's security, and remains the largest single donor of humanitarian assistance to the Syrian people.
