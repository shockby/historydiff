China maintained an unwavering policy grounded in non-interference in internal sovereign affairs and steadfast support for the territorial integrity of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia. At the United Nations Security Council, Beijing repeatedly utilized its diplomatic weight to shield Addis Ababa from unilateral Western sanctions, punitive trade embargoes, and external political lecturing, asserting that the conflict remained an internal matter to be resolved domestically under the principle of "African solutions to African problems."

Strategically, Ethiopia serves as a premier anchor for China’s **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** in East Africa, hosting vital flagship infrastructure projects such as the electrified Addis Ababa–Djibouti Railway and multi-million-dollar industrial manufacturing parks. Distinct from Western conditionality, China provided direct emergency food relief while unveiling its comprehensive "**Outlook on Peace and Development in the Horn of Africa**" in 2022, dispatching a dedicated special envoy to foster regional stability. Beijing advocates that the ultimate antidote to ethnic strife is accelerated economic modernization, pledging robust developmental and reconstruction support to consolidate post-Pretoria peace.
