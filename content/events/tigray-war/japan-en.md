The Government of Japan viewed the conflict in Tigray with profound concern, recognizing Ethiopia as a cornerstone state indispensable for peace, regional integration, and economic progress in the Horn of Africa. Following the outbreak of hostilities, Tokyo consistently urged an immediate end to military actions, protection of civilians, and the resolution of political differences through peaceful dialogue. Japan officially welcomed the signing of the Pretoria Agreement on November 2, 2022, issuing a Foreign Ministry Press Statement commending the mediation of the African Union and calling upon all signatories to faithfully honor the accord to restore enduring stability.

Guided by its foundational diplomatic commitment to "Human Security," Japan provided extensive emergency humanitarian grant assistance channeled through multilateral partners—including the World Food Programme (WFP), UNICEF, and the International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC)—delivering vital food, therapeutic nutrition, medical supplies, and water rehabilitation to conflict-affected populations across Tigray, Amhara, and Afar. In alignment with the principles of the **Tokyo International Conference on African Development (TICAD)**, Japan champions African ownership in peacebuilding, advocating for sustained reconciliation and socio-economic recovery.
