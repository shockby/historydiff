China is one of the largest users of Red Sea trade routes, and attacks by the **Houthi movement** also harm Chinese trade. However, Beijing criticizes the US-UK military operations as 'unilateral actions,' noting the Houthis' claims of solidarity with Palestinians. China supports UN Security Council ceasefire resolutions and positions its 2023 mediation of the Saudi-Iran reconciliation (Beijing Accord) as a major diplomatic achievement. China continues its involvement in the peace process but has no intention of direct military intervention.
