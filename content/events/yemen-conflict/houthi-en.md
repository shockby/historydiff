As part of Iran's 'Axis of Resistance,' the **Houthi movement (Ansar Allah)** launched attacks on ships in the Red Sea, framing them as actions in solidarity with the Palestinian people in Gaza. Controlling northern Yemen, including Sanaa, they maintain domestic support through anti-imperialist and Islamic resistance narratives. The movement seeks international legitimacy and demands an end to the Saudi-led blockade and airstrikes. They strongly condemn the US and UK airstrikes as blatant Western aggression.
