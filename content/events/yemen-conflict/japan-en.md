For Japan, the Red Sea and Yemen region are critical maritime routes for oil tankers and LNG transport. Attacks on shipping in the Red Sea by the **Houthi movement** have a direct impact on Japan's energy security. The expansion of escort operations by the JMSDF is being cautiously debated due to constitutional constraints. Japan strongly supports the UN-led peace process diplomatically and continues to provide humanitarian assistance through organizations like WFP and UNICEF. Ensuring freedom of navigation in the Red Sea and regional stability are top priorities.
