The United States re-designated the **Houthi movement** as a Specially Designated Global Terrorist (SDGT) entity in 2024. Ensuring freedom of navigation in the Red Sea is deemed essential for economic interests and solidarity with Israel, leading to joint US-UK airstrikes on Houthi military targets inside Yemen under Operation Prosperity Guardian. The US balances restoring relations with Saudi Arabia with pressure diplomacy on Iran. While being the largest humanitarian aid contributor to Yemen, the US continues controversial arms sales to Saudi Arabia.
